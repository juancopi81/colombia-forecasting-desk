## Corrected reader brief

**Nothing needs you today.**

Davivienda opens applications for Colombia’s hiring incentive. Eight Antioquia municipalities vote on Sunday.

- **CREA EMPLEO hiring incentive:** The November 25 amendment sets June 2025 as the first payroll benchmark. Employers with more than 50 dependent workers that month must certify that two-year net sales growth did not exceed the prior year’s inflation. Davivienda lists applications for its employer clients from November 26 at 8 a.m. through December 2 at 4 p.m. Affected: Employers adding eligible formal jobs for women, young people or people over 50. [Source](https://comunicaciones.davivienda.com/subsidios-empresas/).

- **Sunday vote in Valle de San Nicolás:** 383,952 eligible voters across eight Antioquia municipalities can vote on creating a metropolitan area on November 29, from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. Each municipality needs a majority in favor and at least 5% turnout. Affected: Residents of the eight municipalities. [Source](https://www.registraduria.gov.co/Este-domingo-29-de-noviembre-se-realizara-la-consulta-popular-para-la.html).

- **Dollar costs more pesos:** The official rate rose from COP 3,592.03 yesterday to COP 3,630.89 today. It is up 9.14% in seven days and 14.98% in 30 days. Fixed dollar payments require more pesos. Affected: People and businesses paying in dollars. [Source](https://www.datos.gov.co/Econom-a-y-Finanzas/Tasa-de-Cambio-Representativa-del-Mercado-TRM/32sa-8pi3).

**Coming up**
